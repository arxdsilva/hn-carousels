Do you know why your last PR should merge?


#3 on HackerNews last week: Colin Breck was arguing that a generated list of changed files rarely tells a reviewer what matters.


My rule: write the why yourself, in a few sentences, then let AI check that description against the diff for missed details. The model can catch a mistake, but it can't tell your reviewer why you made the change, because it never knew.
