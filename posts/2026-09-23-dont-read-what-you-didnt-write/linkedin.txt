Do you know why your last PR should merge?

Engineer Colin Breck argues AI-generated PR descriptions and design docs are becoming unreadable because whoever prompted the model already had the full context in their head and skipped writing it down. A generated list of changed files rarely tells a reviewer what matters.

My rule: write the reasoning yourself, in a few plain sentences, then let AI check that description against the diff for typos or missed details. The model can catch a mistake, but it can't tell your reviewer why you made the change, because it never knew.
