How Strata runs a 125-billion-parameter model on one gaming GPU.

It only loads the slice of the model each token actually needs.
---
1 MoE: only a few of 24,576 experts fire per token.
2 Offloading: hot experts stay on the GPU, cold ones wait in RAM or on disk.
3 Quantization: shrinks each expert to 2 or 3-bit, trading accuracy for speed.
