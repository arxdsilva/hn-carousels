Myth: the safest dependency is the one published 3 hours ago.

#5 on HackerNews this week: Rust 1.100 shipped dependency cooldowns in Cargo.

Malicious versions are usually caught within hours or days, by scanners or the community, the Rust Release Team writes. Pull a release on day one and you skip that check. With a min-publish-age set, Cargo skips anything newer and tells you what it held back.

It has limits. cargo install ignores the setting, and the Rust team says cooldowns are no complete defense against supply chain attacks. An urgent fix still goes through with a one-off override.

My rule: give new versions a few days before they reach your build, and keep the override for urgent fixes. Outside Rust, Renovate's minimumReleaseAge or Dependabot's cooldown setting does the same job.
