Anthropic just rediscovered the monorepo problem Google solved a decade ago. Their coding agents pushed git past its limits.
---
In May they merged 2,500 changes a day. By September, over 30,000. Git's distributed design couldn't keep up with that.
---
The fix isn't new. Google built Piper for this. Meta built Sapling. Centralized, always-online source control for massive scale.
---
Scion assumes every client stays online. Git assumes the opposite: offline-first, every clone a full copy. That's what broke.
---
Developers in the discussion pushed back: this scale of churn only makes sense at Anthropic's size, not advice for most teams.
