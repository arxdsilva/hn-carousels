TV4 just rediscovered the oldest bug in production pipelines: an untested backup is not a backup.
---
A Swedish TV production lost the final cut of its show. The premiere is now pushed to 2027. TV4 only confirmed a "technical failure," not that an AI agent caused it.
---
This isn't new. Teams keep backups for years without ever running a real restore test. The first real test lands the day you need it, and by then it's too late.
---
The fix is boring: put restore drills on the calendar, same as deploys. A backup nobody has restored is a hope, not a plan.
---
Per one engineer in the discussion, AI or not, the fault sits with whoever skipped testing the recovery process, not the tool that triggered the failure.
