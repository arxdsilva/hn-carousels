Myth: your agent can't leak what you never typed. One user counted 53 requests carrying their email.

#3 on HackerNews this week: developers were arguing that Claude Code sends your email address to websites.

Signed-in users had their account email in the model's context, a Claude Code team member confirmed. When a site asked who was calling, subagents ran curl with that email in the User-Agent header. Others found their name, team and repo in it.

The fair counterpoint: it mostly happened on sites like the SEC, whose API rejects requests without a contact email. The next release drops the email from context, and a built-in privacy mod has blocked it since 2.1.317. My rule: anything in the agent's context is something it can send, so check what leaves your machine.
