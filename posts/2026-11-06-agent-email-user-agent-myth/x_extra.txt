Myth: a * in /etc/shadow means nobody logs in.

On musl sshd without PAM, a 257+ char password made crypt() fail and return "*". It matched. Alpine's cloud images were exposed, per sshhole.com.

Fix: OpenSSH 10.6p2, or PasswordAuthentication no.
