Goodfire says roughly 90% of neurons in Qwen3-32B have one main job.

Per Nick Cammarata, the "tangled" neurons the field blamed on superposition came from ordinary problems you can fix.
---
What they changed:
1 Broader, longer-context data. Short contexts made long-context neurons look mixed.
2 Describe the pattern a neuron shares, often "better understood as a verb than a noun." The cat and car neurons were shape detectors.
---
3 Better metrics. Reward explanations that are right over ones that only simulate activations.
4 Modern architecture. Residual connections and LayerNorm stop deep layers overflowing. He also reports 99-100% of a retrained InceptionV1 variant's last layer is easy to read.
---
He calls it a working hypothesis, and the audit numbers move with the thresholds. One researcher in the discussion wouldn't rush to call superposition wrong. Others just wanted a shorter version: it's a 65-minute read.
---
I care because a model I can read is one I can debug, the way I debug code. One developer in the discussion called that the main reason open weights matter.
