Keeping old links from being hijacked: good, better, best.

Someone re-registered Bitly links in 2011 Burger King and McDonald's tweets. Their accounts were never touched. The link just has a new owner.
---
Good: Bitly. The vendor decides where it points.
Better: short links on your own domain. A stale CNAME to a lapsed domain can be bought.
Best: plain redirects on your main site, in your repo. Keep the domain renewed.
---
Per Ransomnews, 56 of 97 Bitly links in old Trump tweets were created after the tweets. Bitly's help pages say a back-half can't be reused. Bitly has since confirmed the issue and is disabling the affected links.
