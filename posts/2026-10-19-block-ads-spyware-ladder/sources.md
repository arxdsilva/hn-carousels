Structure: ladder
Angle: How to keep ads from becoming a spyware path: good: an ad blocker in every browser, better: turn off or reset the mobile ad ID (MDM on work devices), best: block ads at the gateway or home router.

- HN thread: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50101813 (185 points, 30 comments, rank #10 on the 2026-10-15 front page; 187 points when drafted).
- Article: [Exposing AdFection: How Discover-Tech Turned Targeted Ads Into a Pipeline for Zero-Click Mercenary Spyware](https://citizenlab.ca/research/how-discover-tech-turned-targeted-ads-into-a-pipeline-for-spyware/) by Bill Marczak, Wolfie Christl, John Scott-Railton, Astrid Perry, Siena Anstis and Ron Deibert (citizenlab.ca).
- Rungs: the report's section 11 recommendations (ad-blocking extension / privacy-respecting browser; disable or reset the mobile advertising ID and opt out of ad targeting, via MDM on company devices; block online advertising at the gateway). Section 10: "the most feasible protection is for defenders to block targeted ads at the gateway."
- Counterpoint (thread): one developer noted a big gap between "ad network delivers JS" and a zero-click exploit landing a DLL on the victim's machine, which the report doesn't fully close.
