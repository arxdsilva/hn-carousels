How to stop ads from delivering spyware: good, better, best.

Per Citizen Lab, Candiru spyware reached Al Jazeera devices through targeted ads. Rendering the ad was enough to infect a device.
---
Good: an ad blocker in every browser.
Better: turn off or reset the mobile ad ID, via MDM on work phones.
Best: block ads at the gateway, or on your home router.

All three are the report's own recommendations.
---
One gap a developer raised in the discussion: the report shows an ad network delivering JavaScript, but doesn't fully show how that turned into a DLL on the victim's machine.
