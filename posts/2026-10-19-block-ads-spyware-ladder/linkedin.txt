3 ways to stop ad spyware. Your browser extension is the weakest.

#10 on HackerNews last week: spyware reaching devices once a targeted ad rendered.

To protect your devices:

1. Good: an ad blocker in every browser, but it never sees the ads inside apps.

2. Better: turn off the mobile ad ID, through MDM on work phones. Citizen Lab says this directly blocks the vendor's current method, but the ads still load.

3. Best: block ads at the gateway, or on the router at home. It covers apps too, but not phones that leave the network.

My rule: block ads where the traffic leaves the network, and push the same filter to every work phone, so nobody's safety depends on an extension they remembered to install. At home that's a DNS filter on the router, like Pi-hole, AdGuard Home or NextDNS.
