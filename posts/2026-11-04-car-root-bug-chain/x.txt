Patching the update won't root this car. Neither will its shell command.

Rasmus Moorats just wanted to change the clock on his 2014 Renault. Without the right DRM'd map SD card, the settings menu doesn't even exist. So he rooted it.
---
Plan A, modify the update image: the unit refused it. The image carried a signature he couldn't forge.
---
Plan B, the script engine's shell command: one token, no arguments, and every writable mount was noexec. He could only run binaries already on the read-only rootfs.
---
What worked: a path traversal in the LOG command wrote "|/bin/sh payload.sh" into /proc/sys/kernel/core_pattern. Then he ran dbus-monitor, which aborts with no system bus. The kernel ran his script as root.
---
The actual fix: one number in settings.dat. UTC offset 180 minutes, changed to 120.

One developer in the discussion said he should have just bought the SD card. He replied the old one was already in the trash, and a replacement was about 60 EUR.
