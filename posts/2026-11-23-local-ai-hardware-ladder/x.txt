Running a coding model at home: good, better, best.

Open models like Qwen and Inkling 2 Mini now fit on one graphics card. Your card's memory decides which one you can run.
---
Good: a used 24GB Tesla P40, about $300. Slow, fiddly setup.
Better: a 16GB card with an MoE model partly in system RAM. Low-bit versions feel worse.
Best: a 32GB card or unified-memory Mac, 27B model at 4-bit. Costs thousands.
---
My rule: rent a GPU by the hour on RunPod or Vast.ai and run your real tasks before you buy any card.
