How to run AI models at home: good, better, best. Don't start at best.

#7 on HackerNews this weekend: developers arguing over what hardware a local coding model really needs.

To run one on your own machine:

1. Good: a used 24GB Tesla P40, about $300 according to one developer, but it runs a 27B model at around 14 tokens a second and needs extra setup to work.

2. Better: a 16GB card running a mixture-of-experts model with the rest in system RAM, which Jerry James's calculator puts near 47 tokens a second, but low-bit versions feel noticeably worse than the full model.

3. Best: a 32GB card or a unified-memory Mac running a 27B model at 4-bit with a long context, but the hardware costs thousands.

My rule: rent a GPU by the hour on RunPod or Vast.ai and run your real tasks through LM Studio or llama.cpp before you buy any card.
