Three governments. Same operating system. No coordination.


#8 on HackerNews this week: developers were arguing that swapping the OS is the easy part, the office suite is where these migrations stall.


The Dutch government's DAWO project, France's Securix, and Germany's openDesk all landed on the same architecture independently: describe every machine as code, and it builds the same system every time. One engineer in the thread put cloning Microsoft 365 at roughly 80% parity, at best.


If your company dropped a core vendor tomorrow, which would break first: the infrastructure underneath it, or the daily habits built around the tool itself?


Source: DAWO by the DAWO project (dawo.community).
