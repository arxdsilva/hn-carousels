How to make a web page print well: good, better, best.

Brennen Puth hit Ctrl-P on his own blog and got oversized text and gray links. Ctrl-P is the PDF export your users already have.
---
Good: do nothing. The nav and gray links print too.
Better: an @media print block. Hide the nav, 12pt text, black links. Code blocks still split.
Best: add break-inside: avoid and render PDFs from the same page in headless Chrome. Backgrounds still drop.
---
The pushback in the discussion: one developer said print support sits low on their list, below on-screen UX and accessibility. Another replied that it's exactly why they do it from day one, since it heads off the "can we get a PDF?" request.
