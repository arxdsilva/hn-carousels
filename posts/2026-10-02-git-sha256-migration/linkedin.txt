Unpopular opinion: Git's SHA-256 migration won't make your repos safer.

Scott Chacon, GitHub's co-founder, calls Git's SHA-256 default a costly mistake.
He says it defends against an attack nobody has pulled off.
He's right. Real breaches hijack packages, they don't crack hashes.
He's wrong to call it pure waste.
FIPS 140-2 already forces this migration at some firms, exploit or not.

Team Chacon ("not needed"), or Team Compliance ("no choice")? Pick a side.
