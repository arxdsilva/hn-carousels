Keeping up with AI-found bugs: good, better, best.

Postgres 19 is late because LLMs find bugs faster than committers fix them. Back-patched commits rose more than 60% in a year, per Postgres developer Robert Haas.
---
Good: triage by severity with a cut-off. Niche bugs stay open forever.
Better: pause feature work until the backlog shrinks. The roadmap stalls.
Best: run the bug hunt on every PR before merge. Old code isn't covered, and fixes still need a human.
---
The pushback in the discussion: discovery stopped being the bottleneck, verification didn't. More reports don't mean faster fixes when the same few maintainers still review every one.
