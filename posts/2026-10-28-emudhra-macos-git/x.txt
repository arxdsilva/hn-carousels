Updating macOS won't fix your git. Neither will fixing it once.

Per Dennis Jackson, the git, curl and python3 Apple ships still trust eight compromised eMudhra certs, ten days after Apple was told. Anyone on your network path can fake GitHub.
---
Plan A, wait for Apple: macOS 27.2 shipped on Oct 26 without the fix. Safari is protected. The command-line tools aren't.
---
Plan B, remove the roots by hand: 27.2 undid that workaround, and Apple's own guidance says updates may revert it.
---
What works: remove them, then rerun the one-line git ls-remote check from his page after every update. Do it on macOS CI runners too.
---
One developer in the discussion said: move git remotes to SSH, this will keep happening. Another replied that SSH just trades CAs for blindly trusting the host key on first connect.
