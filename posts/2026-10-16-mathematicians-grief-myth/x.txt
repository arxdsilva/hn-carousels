Myth: mathematicians crying over AI is just bruised ego.

Derek Thompson grants some of it is. He argues they also mourn the human conversation of discovery, which OpenAI's proofs are "interrupting."

I think programmers face the same question. Grief or ego?
---
The best counter from developers in the discussion: it's "almost all bruised egos and feeling of wasted time." Another pointed out that software engineers losing jobs to AI get far less sympathy than mathematicians do.
